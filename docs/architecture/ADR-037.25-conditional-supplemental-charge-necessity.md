# ADR-037.25 - Conditional necessity of supplemental charging

Status: ACCEPTED - Alex authorized the scoped repair on 19 October 2026.
Supplements ADR-037.9/.10/.24 and V2ADR-064.7. Earlier decisions remain frozen.

## Decision

A supplemental charge assignment remains binding while its bridge energy need
is demonstrably present and its own target has not been measured as completed.
An unstarted assignment may be retired only through a selected, fully simulated
replacement route proving that its bridge need no longer exists. Retirement is
not completion. Preserve the old identity and evidence in durable history.

This applies only to supplemental household charging. It does not authorize
canceling or weakening market routes, export budgets, trading commitments or
daily main-charge targets. Existing market obligations participate unchanged in
the physical proof. Running charging retains its existing protection.
Within an existing market-revision search, supplemental retirement is considered
only for the seed retaining the incumbent's exact export intervals and targets.
Changed market seeds keep their prior authority and do not gain this additional
retirement choice. Compare exports to the incumbent, not merely to the seed.

## Responsibilities

- Planning Input supplies evidence, not cancellation decisions.
- The reference adapter proves reserve and bridge feasibility on the proposed
  complete trajectory. SOC clamped at the minimum is insufficient proof: use
  the existing energy-deficit assessment, including conversion losses.
- Candidate Generation considers future supplemental release alongside main
  route revision and retains the original alternative. A retired assignment's
  original command segments must not survive as anonymous retained charging.
- Evaluation remains the sole selector and uses existing economic rules. No
  fixed priority or new price threshold favors removal over necessary charging.
- Plan Builder and Store atomically publish the winning route and explicit
  retirement record. Store validates identity, lifecycle and segment consistency;
  it does not discover or economically choose a cancellation.
- Execution proves actual completion. It neither invents energy needs nor
  independently removes commitments.

A still-needed goal may be rebound only to a real charging interval, not an
unrelated declining NOM interval whose starting SOC already exceeds its target.
A missed goal is not itself evidence of a new physical energy deficit.
Rebinding must not leave its original grid-charge command running anonymously:
every surviving interval of that command remains explicitly owned by a charge
goal or a daily main route.

Discovery is bounded: retain all goals, release each eligible goal individually,
and release all eligible goals together. This is not an exhaustive search of
every subset. Main-route discovery can compensate elsewhere only when the
complete replacement remains physically feasible and economically preferable.

When a main-route comparison includes supplemental retirement, evaluate full
horizon import and export cash flows in EUR for every alternative, including
the incumbent. Require the same horizon and terminal stored energy; do not
invent a residual battery valuation. This conservative comparability requirement
may exclude otherwise feasible routes with different terminal stock. Ordinary
main-only and market-route comparisons keep their existing economic basis.

## Verification contract

Prove optional retirement with unchanged market obligations and daily goals;
preserve required and running charging; reject unsupported Store omissions;
retain retirement after restart without fabricated completion; and replay the
recorded 18 October planning observation against reconstructed ownership state.
Publication and
live installation are separate from local verification.
