# V2ADR-064.10 — Continuous post-goal solar window

Date: 2026-10-17. Status: local implementation; release separate.

Alex reported the fragmented daytime NOM/discharge-only schedule in dev.303 and
approved a continuous solar window. V2ADR-064.9 chose individual surplus intervals
too literally: NOM already supports household demand during temporary deficits.

After the daily goal, free household-support intervals between the first and last
forecast surplus of that delivery day become NOM. The configured Solcast basis and
total demand, including excluded EV demand, still determine the window boundaries.
There is no extension into the night or another delivery day. Protected intervals,
explicit grid charging, standby and market export remain unchanged and may split
the solar window. EV runtime protections remain authoritative.

This changes the actual candidate schedule and its simulation before evaluation,
not merely the displayed chart. It neither extends the owned main charge nor
changes financial priorities. It adds no grid-charge action.

Regression evidence: the two initially-full/early-full continuous-window checks
fail on dev.303 and pass with this change. Coverage includes protected gaps and
standby/grid/export actions inside the window, forecast-basis selection, EV demand,
and a recalculation/persistence/projection test requiring one resulting NOM segment.
