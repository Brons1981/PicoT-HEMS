# V2ADR-064.11 - Vehicle charge-end confirmation inside Energy Devices

Status: implemented locally; not released or installed.

Energy Devices may read the configured `ev_vehicle_state_entity`, defaulting to
`sensor.wba5x71010fj59919_charging_ev_charging_state`. An empty value disables this
optional input. HEMS receives no BMW entity or state and retains no device knowledge.

BavarianData's `descriptor_metadata.py` for the charging status descriptor lists
`chargingactive`, `chargingended`, `chargingpaused`, `charginginterrupted`,
`chargingdisrupted` and `chargingerror` separately. Its descriptor reference describes
`CHARGINGENDED` as charging ended, not proof of full SOC. Fullness belongs to a separate
HV status descriptor (`finished_fully_charged`). ED therefore labels this reason
"BMW heeft laden beëindigd (geen bewijs van 100%)".

Source inspected 2026-10-18:
https://github.com/JustChr/BavarianData/blob/main/custom_components/bavariandata/descriptor_metadata.py
https://github.com/JustChr/BavarianData/blob/main/tools/descriptor-list.csv

Completion requires a chargingactive event within this session and the authorized
window, followed by a later chargingended event, both observed within five minutes
of HA last_changed. The plug must still be on, without a pending stop or manual pause.
Prior-session state, unknown/unavailable state, paused/error/interrupted state and
switch-off do not establish completion. Active evidence persists across restart but
is discarded on sensor change, interrupted states or switch-off.

The existing switch executor persists and retries the stop command, then records
vehicle_charging_ended after observing the plug off. Estimated remaining energy is
not falsified to zero. The accepted deadline remains a hard stop and never extends
automatically. Dashboard history distinguishes vehicle completion, low-power stop,
cancelled execution and scheduled_deadline. An unavailable BMW sensor leaves existing
meter and deadline handling intact.
