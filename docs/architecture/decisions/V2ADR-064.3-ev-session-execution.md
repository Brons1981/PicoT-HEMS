# V2ADR-064.3 — Confirmed EV session demand and charger execution

Status: Accepted for the explicitly authorized experimental implementation, 2026-10-09.

Extends V2ADR-064 and V2ADR-064.2 under the user's request to add EV session recognition, scheduling, smartplug execution and canonical HEMS ingestion. Original ADR-001–037 and the canonical planning chain remain authoritative. The older producer-only/no-services restriction remains applicable to ordinary device learning; this slice explicitly grants Energy Devices authority over one user-selected EV switch. It grants no storage actuation, vendor automation replacement, planner or tariff-selection authority.

## Ownership and admission

Energy Devices owns the session, its durable identity, physical charger observations, user-confirmed start and duration, and execution of that switch. The configured meter is physical truth. As clarified by the user on 9 October, switch on plus a new valid physical power report above 2000 W establishes recognition immediately. The report must not predate the switch-on timestamp and may be at most 75 seconds old; the original thirty seconds were practical waiting time, not an algorithmic admission condition. Brief low-power pauses remain the same session. Five minutes of continuous, available low-power observations establish natural completion. Missing observations cannot establish completion. A user-confirmed end time is also a hard execution deadline, reported separately from measured completion. Energy is integrated from power, with gap and uncertainty evidence; it is an estimate, not a calibrated cumulative meter reading. Recognition copies measured power directly; duration remains unknown until the user supplies it. No unseen BMW data is invented or treated as current.

Turning the switch off leaves planned demand intact. Manual off during an owned run pauses execution; only explicit resume re-enables it. The user must first verify the vehicle resumes after off/on. Confirmation is bound to the configured switch/meter pair. Commands are recorded before dispatch, retried with a ten-second interval, and acknowledged by observed switch state. A service response alone does not prove charging. The confirmed deadline is respected after process restart; an expired session is stopped, never started late.

## Snapshot

The independent `energy-device-session-demand:v1` snapshot is published at `sensor.picot_ev_session_snapshot`. It carries source entity IDs, session ID/version/state, confirmed start/end, expected power/duration, remaining/observed energy, actual power, original measurement timestamp, availability and uncertainty. A constant power profile is the initial bounded forecast. Stable measured power changes refine this profile; routine delivered-energy countdown is not itself a material transition. Recognized but unconfirmed sessions have no accepted forecast. Cancelled/completed sessions remove the contribution. Replacement and cancellation never accumulate older session contributions.

HEMS accepts a fresh (15-second) generated snapshot through its existing source reader and Planning Input. It keeps physical RAW facts intact and annotates separately identified EV power for the residual household-history view. While a confirmed session forecast is admitted, historical observations without proven EV coverage are not used as a residual baseline. Proven attributed observations are subtracted time-aligned, and missing residual coverage uses the existing conservative fallback and live guard. Ordinary operation without a confirmed session retains the existing history model. No historical EV power is invented from a label or a thirty-second probe.

The session forecast is independent of battery exclusion. Exclusion is admitted only with a separate fresh, enabled regulation policy; disabling correction must never leave fictional battery protection in the simulator. Projected demand is bounded by both remaining energy and the confirmed execution deadline. With unavailable correction, storage may support EV demand in the simulation because the physical original regulator can do so.

Session ID, state, start, duration, support policy and persistent changes of at least 100W enter the existing thirty-second materiality observation producer. Revision/heartbeat and normal energy countdown do not. Runtime Monitor → canonical MEP → Evaluation → Plan Builder remains the sole planning route. No session API launches MEP or chooses a battery mode.

## Rollback and verification

Both add-on session execution and HEMS ingestion default off. Disable scheduled execution only after cancelling/stopping the owned session; otherwise losing the producer cannot guarantee a future stop. Independent P1 RAW fallback remains in Home Assistant and is not changed here. Original Gielz remains intact. Offline service-failure, restart, cancellation, timestamp, noise and canonical ingestion regressions are required. Live BMW resume, charger switching, actual HEMS plans and battery protection remain separate practical acceptance tests.
