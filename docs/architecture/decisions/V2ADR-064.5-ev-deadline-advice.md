# V2ADR-064.5 — HEMS EV deadline advice

Status: locally implemented; not released or installed.
Extends V2ADR-064.4's second, advisory slice. Baseline: HEMS dev.299 and
Energy Devices 0.4.0-dev.6 (main 25146b0b649212826a63e1b3c7828609f45a55e0).

## User outcome

Energy Devices accepts a local ready-by date/time and estimated remaining
grid-side kWh, using the saved fixed charging power. The request can span seven
days. Clicking request declares availability; BMW plug recognition is not a gate.
No BMW SOC entities are invented. The estimate includes charger losses and does
not prove a full battery. An open physical session blocks a hypothetical request.

The dashboard displays HEMS's recommended contiguous charging block and estimated
incremental household cost. Unknown later prices or missing planning coverage
prevent a claim of a globally cheapest schedule. This slice does not execute
advice, reserve future EV capacity, pause/resume the vehicle or provide a local
latest-start guard. Nu laden retains its existing physical behavior.

## Separate contracts and ownership

Energy Devices persists `energy-device-charging-request:v1` separately from
confirmed `energy-device-session-demand:v1` physical demand. Its session UUID,
revision, available_from, ready_by, grid-side Wh, W and evidence source/time are
immutable within a revision. Edits increment the revision; cancellation increments
and invalidates it. A new physical session supersedes the request. Device-pair
changes clear it. Request creation never authorizes the switch.

A separate 15-second exchange publishes `sensor.picot_ev_charging_request` and
reads `sensor.picot_ev_charging_advice`. HEMS's worker is enabled under the existing
energy_device_sessions_enabled option, submits at most once per 30 seconds and
holds at most one calculation, with no queue. It reads a fresh request envelope
and an immutable canonical snapshot, then rechecks request identity after
calculation. It has no commitment-store or actuator capability. Slow transport
and advice calculations are outside the one-second EV control loop.

The returned `energy-device-charging-advice:v1` carries request identity/revision,
snapshot/run identity, input time, assessment status, coverage, selected slot,
required/scheduled energy and estimated incremental costs. Energy Devices rejects
wrong identity, cancelled/revised requests, future timestamps, older replies and
replies older than five minutes. The UI marks retained advice expired after that
time. A failed exchange cannot make stale advice executable.

## Canonical economic assessment

HEMS produces up to 200 contiguous placement candidates from known tariff edges,
earliest availability and exact latest finish. This bounded candidate set is not
an exhaustive mathematical optimization of all real-valued start times or split
charging schedules. Energy Devices contains no tariff ranking.

Each candidate overlays a copied household forecast with exactly one EV demand,
split at its actual boundaries. Existing external EV demand blocks hypothetical
placement rather than being counted twice. The current retained battery route
is simulated through IndependentDailyReferenceAdapter, validated with its existing
committed-execution checks and settled through IndependentDailyFinancialSettlement
and IndependentDailyTariffAdapter. The existing EvaluationEngine selects the
financial winner. CanonicalPipeline.run is deliberately not called: even a
control-disabled canonical run can persist commitments.

The initial advice reserves battery support for the existing plan: EV demand is
battery-excluded, and a candidate may not reduce stored energy at any original
baseline checkpoint. This also rejects diversion of PV needed by a committed
battery route. A missing, ambiguous or invalid retained plan blocks advice.
No baseline forecast, plan or commitment is mutated.

Only contiguous published tariff coverage is priced. The physical validation
still covers the retained plan. If the deadline extends beyond assessed coverage,
a feasible known block is explicitly provisional; insufficient known coverage is
blocked, distinct from a physically impossible deadline. All arithmetic uses
offset-aware UTC instants, including elapsed time around DST transitions.

## Verification and remaining gates

Focused tests exercise durable requests, no switch dispatch, edit/cancel races,
real HTTP handler parsing, the request-to-real-HEMS-to-dashboard return path,
canonical price/PV comparisons, unchanged commitments, partial price evidence,
infeasible deadlines and invalid or stale input. Static typing, lint and dashboard
JavaScript syntax are checked separately.

Live HA transport, release packaging and actual BMW resumption are installation
gates. Automatic execution remains a later slice with an explicit schedule
acceptance contract, continuity/minimum on-off rules and latest-start behavior.
