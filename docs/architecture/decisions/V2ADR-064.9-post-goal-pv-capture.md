# V2ADR-064.9 — PV self-consumption after daily completion

Date: 2026-10-17. Status: implemented locally; release separate.

## Decision

The once-per-delivery-day full-storage goal is not a limit on later PV charging.
After that goal's main charge is trimmed at its first proven completion, retain
PV capture in later free intervals of the same delivery day when the configured
planning PV basis exceeds total expected consumption (including excluded EV load).

These intervals are NOM in the complete candidate schedule and physical projection,
but are not owned main-charge segments. They therefore appear in the execution
plan and SOC expectation without extending the daily obligation or its acquisition
price window. They cannot add grid charging. Existing NOM, standby, explicit grid
charging, export and protected intervals retain their existing behavior; intervals
outside this delivery day remain untouched.

This is the requested self-consumption behavior, not a claim that storage always
beats export financially. The financial objective order, tariff policy, market
admission, terminal-inventory comparison and EV execution guard are unchanged.

## Cause and scope

Explicit rebuilding releases free intervals to household-support-only. Discovery
previously discarded every owned interval after the daily target, including later
PV capture. With an early grid charge the selected plan could consequently lose
all daytime capture. Normal incremental revisions often hid this by retaining
existing daytime NOM.

The fix adds passive capture only when admitting a completed main-charge candidate.
The charge-window search, full-target proof and protected running prefix are kept.
Existing committed plans require the ordinary explicit recalculation to use it;
there is no live migration or automatic repricing introduced here.

## Evidence

- Regression checks cover early grid completion and initially full storage, physical
  PV-to-storage energy, no extra grid charge, unchanged main ownership, selected
  forecast basis, EV demand, and protected/standby/grid/export intervals.
- Pipeline regression covers explicit recalculation, durable plan capture, restored
  SOC projection and unchanged repeated-poll behavior.
- Offline canonical replay of `picot-diagnostics - 2026-10-17T201402.426.zip`
  keeps grid charging at 04:15–05:45 Europe/Amsterdam on 18 October. Later free
  surplus intervals become NOM. Expected PV-to-storage input is 1445.54 Wh and
  terminal SOC is 63.05%, versus 47.01% without PV capture.

No release, deployment or live device action is part of this change.
